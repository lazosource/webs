LAZO CHAT
=========
See your Twitch chat on top of Rust while you play.

START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (like your Desktop).
2. Double-click "LAZO Chat.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   (It says that because the app isn't signed, not because something is wrong.)
3. Type your Twitch name, then click SAVE & LOCK.

IN RUST
Set Options -> Graphics -> Display mode to "Borderless Windowed".
In normal Fullscreen, Windows hides anything on top of the game.

KEYS
Ctrl+Shift+L   Edit: move, resize, change name, text size, background, fade
Ctrl+Shift+H   Hide / show chat
Ctrl+Shift+Q   Quit

When locked, clicks go straight through the chat to your game.
There's also a small "L" icon by your clock: click it for the same options.
