Security Topics

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails and messages that trick you into giving up passwords or money.

🦠 Malware

Viruses, ransomware, and spyware designed to damage or steal from your devices.

🔐 Encryption

Scrambling data so only authorized people can read it.

🌐 Network Security

Firewalls, VPNs, and safe Wi-Fi practices to protect your traffic.

🕵️ Social Engineering

Manipulating people, not machines, to break security.

🧪 Ethical Hacking

Legally testing systems to find weaknesses before criminals do.

Quick Safety Tips

  • Use long, unique passwords and a password manager.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) everywhere.
  • Keep your system and apps updated.
  • Never click suspicious links or open unknown attachments.
  • Back up your important files regularly.
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive activity, or use a VPN.
